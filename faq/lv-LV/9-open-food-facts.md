## What is the difference with other web sites, services and mobile applications that already allow to view food products information?

Galvenā atšķirība mums ir kritiska: mūsu dati ir brīvi pieejami visiem un visiem lietojumiem. Tas ir tā, ko sauc par atvērtiem datiem.

Gandrīz visas citas vietnes, pakalpojumi un lietojumprogrammas aizliedz citiem reproducēt un atkārtoti izmantot savus datus. Gluži pretēji, viņi to greizsirdīgi patur sev. In almost all cases, their terms of service explicitly forbid any non-personal use and any extraction of all the data or parts of the data.

Mēs uzskatām, ka informācija par pārtikas produktiem ir pārāk svarīga un noderīga, lai to glabātu seifā. So when we started the Open Food Facts project and mobile app, back in 2012, we decided to do exactly the opposite: not only we allow use and reuse of our database, freely and without fee, to everyone and for all uses (including commercial), but we also encourage it!

Making the data publicly available (what is known as _open data_) allows individuals, associations, companies, researchers etc. from all around the world to think up and develop applications for the data that we certainly would never have thought about.

## What is Open Food Facts' economic model

Atbalstītāji ir brīvprātīgie. Viņu iemaksas tiek apkopotas atklātā datubāzē, kuru var izmantot ikviens un visiem lietojumiem. (see the [Terms of reuse](https://en.openfoodfacts.org/terms-of-use#reuse))

Everyone (including but not limited to Open Food Facts contributors and creators) can thus redistribute and/or reuse the data to build web sites, services, software, mobile applications, or to write articles and studies. Viņi var brīvi padarīt iegūto darbu brīvi pieejamu vai pārdot vai gūt peļņu (piemēram, ar reklāmām), ja vien tie ievēro atkārtota izmantošanas noteikumus.

We are also strictly independent from the food industry, and all the services and software we build are free. For example, our Platform for Producers is totally free, and we are all the more pleased with that because we think it might help them improve their products.

## Can I add product pictures or data from the manufacturer's site, shopping sites or other sites?

Droši vien nē. Gandrīz visas pārējās vietnes aizliedz savu datu un attēlu reproducēšanu un atkārtotu izmantošanu, un tieši tāpēc mēs esam izveidojuši Open Food Facts: lai visi šie dati būtu pieejami visiem un visiem lietojumiem.

Lai izvairītos no jebkādām juridiskām problēmām, mēs lūdzam autorus pievienot tikai tos attēlus, kurus viņi paņēma paši, un tikai datus, kas nāk no produkta iepakojuma un etiķetes.

## Is the information and data on products verified?

The information and data is submited by the Open Food Facts contributors. Atbalstītāji arī nosūta attēlus no produkta, tā etiķetes, sastāvdaļu sarakstiem un uztura faktu tabulas. Šaubu gadījumā apmeklētāji paši var pārbaudīt precizitāti, un, ja ir kļūda, viņi to var labot uz vietas.

Vieglāk atklāt iespējamās kļūdas, pakāpeniski pievienosim automatizētas pārbaudes. e.g. if the nutrition facts of a product are very different from products of the same category, it may be an error. Ja ēdieni papildina vairāk nekā 100%, tas noteikti ir kļūda.

Visbeidzot, daudzi ražotāji, izmantojot mūsu ražotāju platformu, sūta mums fotoattēlus un datus par saviem produktiem. If you are a producer, please [contact us](mailto:producers@openfoodfacts.org)

## I am a food product manufacturer, can I add my own products?

Jā! Vienīgais nosacījums ir pieņemt, ka dati un attēli ir pieejami saskaņā ar atklāto licenci. (see the [Terms of contribution](https://en.openfoodfacts.org/terms-of-use#contribution))

We have developped a free platform to let you easily import photos and data about your products, regardless of your size. Tas ļaus jums importēt pilnīgus un atjauninātus datus un vizuālos materiālus platformā Open Food Facts, kas būs redzami vairāk nekā 100 citās uztura lietotnēs. Additionaly, the platform provides suggestions to improve the Nutri-Score of your products. Contact-us to discuss how we could proceed: [producers@openfoodfacts.org](mailto:producers@openfoodfacts.org)

## Who uses Open Food Facts data?

It would
take too long to list all the organizations that rely on the Open Food Facts
platform.

- More than 100 mobile applications
  address the needs of many audiences: diabetes (VeryDiab), allergies, food
  balance via artificial intelligence (FoodVisor), disabilities, and more. Our
  database also allowed the creation of Yuka.
- Lots of websites: CombienDeSucres.fr,
  CestEmballePresDeChezVous.fr...
- Data-journalists : Les Décodeurs du Monde, BFM
  TV
- Researchers in nearly 100 scientific
  articles
- Food banks and solidarity stores: a
  Data For Good project in the continuity of a pilot project initiated by the
  French Red Cross, integration in the cash management of a small food
  cooperative.
- Companies: Terraillon uses our data
  to give life to its connected scales.
- Programming schools (Simplon, Open
  Classrooms) have integrated Open Food Facts as a motivating project in their
  curriculum.
